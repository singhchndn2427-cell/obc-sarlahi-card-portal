About Nepal OBC Kisaan Mahasangh

हाम्रो संस्थागत परिचय

Registered Federation

Nepal OBC Kisaan Mahasangh (नेपाल ओबीसी किसान महासंघ) is an autonomous non-governmental representative body formed to safeguard, unite, and represent farmers belonging to Other Backward Classes (OBC) and agrarian families across Nepal.

Mission Statement

To champion fair crop pricing, timely supply of fertilizers, modernized agricultural infrastructure, and institutional dignity for every OBC farmer.

Strategic Vision

A prosperous, resilient Nepal where agricultural workers hold central socio-economic empowerment and equitable governance representation.
